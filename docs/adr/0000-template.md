# NNNN. Title: a short noun phrase for the decision

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Decided by:** who

## Context

The situation and the forces at play: what problem, what constraints, what we know. Facts with sources. Two to six sentences.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two plain sentences, starting "We will ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now must do, and what would make us revisit this (a trigger, not a date).

Links to the documents, code or research behind it.
